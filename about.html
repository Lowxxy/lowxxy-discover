01 / THE BEGINNING

CREATIVITY CAME FIRST.

ALWAYS
CREATING.

For as long as I can remember, I've been creating. Growing up, I was always drawing, painting, making stop-motion videos, and experimenting with whatever materials I could get my hands on. Creativity was never something I had to force. It was simply how I expressed myself.

But I wasn't interested in art only for how it looked. I loved storytelling, inventing characters, and finding new ways to bring ideas into the real world. I wanted to make things people hadn't seen before—things that could make someone stop, think, or experience something differently.

Fashion was another form of creativity that always interested me. I loved how clothing could communicate personality without saying a word. Still, I never imagined my artistic journey would eventually lead me into streetwear.

02 / THE JOURNEY

LEARNING TO BRING IDEAS TO LIFE.

FROM ART
TO ANIMATION.

At 18, I moved to Orlando, Florida, to attend Full Sail University, where I studied Computer Animation.

There, I learned and collaborated alongside artists, animators, and designers, gaining experience with the processes used throughout the professional animation and visual-effects industries. My education introduced me to the artistic and technical sides of bringing characters to life—from design and 3D modeling to movement, performance, and visual storytelling.

I graduated at 20 with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Animation. What I took away from that experience was more than a degree. It was the ability to turn imagination into something people could see, interact with, and experience. And I knew I wanted to use that ability to create something of my own.

03 / THE WHY

A DIFFERENT KIND OF ORIGINAL.

WHY
LOWXXY EXISTS.

Not long after graduating, I founded LOWXXY. As someone who admired both fashion and animation, I began thinking about the relationship between creativity, identity, and the industries built around them.

Fashion can be an incredible form of self-expression. But sometimes, the conversation surrounding it becomes more about status, exclusivity, labels, and keeping up with trends than the person actually wearing the clothes. I noticed something similar in creative industries, where competition and recognition can sometimes overshadow the passion that brought artists into those spaces in the first place.

I didn't want to create another brand that told people what they needed to buy to be different. I wanted to build something that encouraged people to be different by simply being themselves.

ORIGINALITY DOESN'T NEED TO BE LOUD, EXPENSIVE, OR COMPLICATED TO HAVE MEANING.

Sometimes, the strongest statement is the simplest one. Stand Out. Stay Humble. That became the foundation of LOWXXY.

04 / THE SYMBOL

THE CROWN THAT WAS CROSSED OUT.

YOU DON'T NEED
A CROWN.

The name LOWXXY is inspired by low-key: subtle, understated, and discreet. I wanted the brand to stand for confidence without arrogance, individuality without comparison, and creativity without a need for validation.

That philosophy inspired the logo. I designed a crown crossed out twice to symbolize letting go of ego, superiority, and the idea that being above others is what makes someone valuable.

It's not about rejecting ambition or success. It's about remembering who you are regardless of what you achieve. You don't need a crown to have worth.

05 / THE CHARACTERS

STORIES WITHOUT EVERY ANSWER.

A WORLD
OF CHARACTERS.

As an animator, I wanted LOWXXY to have more than a logo. I wanted it to have a world. Inspired by the simplicity and expressiveness of vintage animation, I created Lowxxy, the brand's central character and the embodiment of humility.

His design reflects the clothing itself: simple forms, bold black-and-white lines, and a recognizable identity without unnecessary complexity. One of his most intentional features is that he has no mouth.

HIS ACTIONS SPEAK LOUDER THAN WORDS.

Opposing him is King Hubris, a larger-than-life frog who represents pride, ego, and the temptation to place yourself above others. Their opposing personalities reflect the balance between confidence and humility.

I intentionally leave much of their story open to interpretation. Humility means something different to everyone, and I want the audience to discover their own meaning through the characters.

06 / THE EXPERIENCE

WHEN THE SHIRT BECOMES A SCREEN.

FUSING ANIMATION
& FASHION.

Even after creating the designs, characters, and message behind LOWXXY, something felt missing. I had built a world around the brand—but I wanted people to experience that world, not simply see it printed on a shirt.

I started asking myself: What if the artwork you wear could actually come to life?

That's how the LOWXXY AR experience began. Using augmented reality, supported shirt designs can be scanned with a phone to reveal short character animations connected to the artwork. It's like watching a little animated film come to life directly from your clothing.

By combining the nostalgia of vintage animation with modern technology, LOWXXY bridges physical streetwear and digital storytelling. It became more than something to wear. It became something to experience.

07 / THE CRAFT

THE HAND BEHIND THE WORK.

MADE BY AN ARTIST.
NOT AN ALGORITHM.

Every LOWXXY character, clothing graphic, and animation is created by me. From the earliest sketches and character concepts to the final designs and frame-by-frame animation, the artwork comes from my own creative process.

No AI-generated artwork. No AI-generated characters. No AI-generated animation.

I believe there's meaning in knowing the art you're wearing came from someone's imagination, practice, and hands. LOWXXY is an independent creative project built around originality, craftsmanship, and the belief that the story behind something matters just as much as the finished product.

WHEN YOU SUPPORT LOWXXY, YOU'RE SUPPORTING AN INDEPENDENT ARTIST AND THE WORLD HE'S BUILDING.

08 / WHAT LOWXXY STANDS FOR

STAND OUT.
STAY HUMBLE.

LOWXXY isn't about being better than anyone else. It's about being yourself while staying grounded in who you are. You don't need to follow every trend. You don't need a crown to have worth. And you don't need to be louder than everyone else to stand out.

— SLYCE BOWEN FOUNDER & CREATIVE DIRECTOR, LOWXXY
